NUSi - Northwestern University Silicon Initiative

Student-led silicon design and fabrication at Northwestern.

NUSi gives students a practical path into chip design and fabrication, from RTL and verification through physical design, cleanroom process development, project documentation, and industry-facing events.

What happens here

From design to fabrication!

Design Division

RTL, ASIC flow, and verification

Build open-source blocks, learn synthesis and timing, write tests, and bring student projects into complete design pipelines.

Fabrication Division

Design Fabrication Equipment

Develop semiconductor tooling and connect classroom device physics to fabrication practices.

ChipChats

Industry events and workshops

Host technical speakers, tool walkthroughs, design reviews, and career conversations with engineers across the semiconductor stack.

Open Projects

Join or propose a project

Contribute to tutorials, emulation rigs, physical design experiments, fabrication notes, and student-led silicon project logs.
